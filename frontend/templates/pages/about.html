About BitGuard

Building safer Bitcoin transactions

BitGuard is our Software Engineering course project. We are second-year undergraduate students at IIIT Lucknow, and we built it to make Bitcoin wallet risk checks simpler, clearer, and easier to act on.

Software Engineering Project 2nd Year · B.Tech IIIT Lucknow

Who we are

A team of second-year college students at IIIT Lucknow, building BitGuard as part of our Software Engineering assignment.

🛠️

What we practised

Requirements, design, teamwork, version control, testing and deployment, applied to a real-world problem instead of a toy example.

🛡️

What it does

Scores a Bitcoin wallet for risk using graph analysis and machine learning, and explains the signals behind the score.

Why we built BitGuard

Our professors asked us to build a complete software system, and we wanted a problem that actually matters. Cryptocurrency fraud causes real financial harm, and Bitcoin transactions are generally irreversible once sent. Recovery is often difficult or impossible. Many malicious wallets also operate for a long time before they are identified, so users may send funds without realizing the risk.

Existing approaches often depend on static blacklists of wallets that have already been caught. Many current tools are also expensive, enterprise-focused, or too manual for ordinary users to rely on at the moment they need to make a transaction decision.

Our goal

The goal of BitGuard is to provide an accessible way to assess Bitcoin wallet risk using graph-based behavioral analysis and structured signals. Instead of only checking whether a wallet appears on a blacklist, BitGuard helps users see whether a wallet shows patterns that may be associated with risky or malicious behavior.

In short, we want wallet safety checks to be faster, clearer, and easier to use before a transaction happens, not after the funds are already gone.

Who BitGuard is for

Everyday or less technical users who want a quick way to check whether a wallet looks safe before sending money, for example an address copied from a website or a message. It can also be useful for small wallets, exchanges and other platforms that want a lightweight risk-scoring tool without adopting a full enterprise blockchain forensics suite.

A note on this project

BitGuard is a student project built for learning. Its risk scores are informational only and should not be treated as financial or legal advice. We welcome feedback, so please reach out through our contact page.
